MRguldMC Rules

Breaking the rules may result in warnings, temporary punishments or permanent bans.

🎮

01. Gameplay

Play fairly and respect the gameplay experience of other players.

🚫

02. No Cheats

Hacked clients, unfair modifications, exploits and cheats are not allowed.

💬

03. Chat

Keep the chat respectful. Do not spam, harass or intentionally annoy other players.

🤝

04. Respect

Treat other players, staff members and the community with respect.

🏠

05. Griefing

Do not destroy, modify or damage another player's builds without permission.

🔥

06. Stealing

Do not steal items, resources or valuables from other players.

💰

07. Economy

Do not exploit bugs or glitches to generate money, items or other economic advantages.

🐛

08. Exploits

Report serious bugs to staff instead of using them to gain an unfair advantage.

📢

09. Advertising

Advertising other Minecraft servers, communities or services is not allowed.

🧑‍💼

10. Staff

Follow reasonable instructions from staff members while they are handling situations.

⚔️

11. PvP

PvP must be agreed upon where required. Do not intentionally abuse PvP mechanics.

🛡️

12. Protection

Do not attempt to bypass server protections, permissions or security systems.

IMPORTANT

Common Sense Applies

Not every possible situation can be covered by a written rule. Staff may take action when behavior clearly damages the community.
